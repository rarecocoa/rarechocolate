Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At Rare Cocoa™, every order is packed with care to ensure your chocolate arrives fresh and intact. Please read our shipping policy to understand delivery timelines, charges, and important information.

1. Processing Time

  • Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days of payment confirmation.
  • Custom or made-to-order products may require up to 3–5 business days for preparation before dispatch.
  • Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays will be processed on the next business day.

2. Delivery Timeframes

  • Within Andhra Pradesh & Telangana: 2–4 business days after dispatch.
  • Rest of India (Metro cities): 4–6 business days after dispatch.
  • Non-metro and remote areas: 5–8 business days after dispatch.

These are estimated timeframes and may vary due to factors beyond our control, such as courier delays, weather, or public holidays.

3. Shipping Charges

  • Shipping charges are calculated at checkout based on your location and order weight.
  • We offer free shipping on orders above ₹999 within India.

4. Packaging & Temperature Sensitivity

Chocolate is a temperature-sensitive product. We use insulated packaging during warmer months to minimise melting risk. However, we recommend storing your order in a cool, dry place immediately upon receipt. We are not responsible for melting due to extreme heat conditions during transit.

5. Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking number via WhatsApp or email. You can use this to track your delivery in real-time with our courier partner.

6. Failed Deliveries

If a delivery attempt fails due to an incorrect address or unavailability, the courier will make up to 2 additional attempts. If all attempts fail, the order will be returned to us and a re-delivery fee will apply.

7. International Shipping

Currently, we only ship within India. International shipping is not available at this time.
