Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Rare Cocoa ("we", "us", or "our") operates the website rarecocoa.com and accepts orders via WhatsApp. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect the personal information you share with us when you visit our site or contact us to place an order.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us, we may collect the following:

  • Your name and contact number (via WhatsApp)
  • Your delivery address
  • UPI payment screenshot shared voluntarily to confirm payment
  • Browser type, device information, and IP address (collected automatically when you visit our website)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To confirm and process your order
  • To coordinate payment confirmation and delivery
  • To respond to queries and provide customer support
  • To improve our website and service

3. Payment Information

All payments are made via UPI. Payment confirmation is shared as a screenshot directly through WhatsApp. We do not collect, store, or process any card or banking credentials. We do not use any payment gateway on our website.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties, except with our delivery partner strictly to fulfill your order (i.e., your name and delivery address).

5. Data Retention

We retain order and contact details only for as long as necessary to fulfill your order and for any follow-up support. You may contact us at any time to request deletion of your data.

6. Cookies

Our website may use cookies for basic functionality and to remember your browsing session. No personal data is collected via cookies. You may disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of any personal data you have shared with us. To exercise these rights, contact us via WhatsApp or email.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised date. Continued use of our website constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
