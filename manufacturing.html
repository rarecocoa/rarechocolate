Behind the Bar

Our Manufacturing Process

From carefully selected cocoa beans to the finished bar in your hands — every step is guided by honesty, patience, and craft. No shortcuts. No compromises.

The Journey

Bean to Bar, Step by Step

1

Sourcing

We begin with single-origin cocoa beans, carefully selected from trusted farms that practice ethical and sustainable agriculture. Every batch is traceable back to its source — because transparency is not optional, it's foundational.

2

Sorting & Cleaning

Each bean is hand-sorted and cleaned to remove any impurities. We take time at this stage — quality can never be rushed. Only the finest beans make it through to the next step.

3

Roasting

Our beans are roasted in small batches at carefully controlled temperatures. This is where the magic begins — the natural flavours of the cocoa develop their depth, complexity, and aroma.

4

Cracking & Winnowing

The roasted beans are cracked open to reveal the cocoa nibs inside. The shells are separated through winnowing, leaving behind pure cocoa nibs — the heart of real chocolate.

5

Grinding & Conching

The nibs are ground into a smooth, liquid chocolate mass. Through extended conching — a slow mixing process — the chocolate develops its signature silky texture and refined flavour profile.

6

Tempering

Precise temperature control during tempering gives our chocolate its satisfying snap, glossy finish, and smooth melt. This is the hallmark of properly made chocolate — and we never skip it.

7

Moulding & Packaging

Finally, the tempered chocolate is poured into moulds, cooled, and hand-wrapped with care. Every bar is inspected before it reaches you — because you deserve nothing less than perfection.

Our Promise

Quality Commitments

Single-Origin Only

Every batch uses cocoa from a single, traceable source. No blends, no anonymity.

Pure Ingredients

Zero preservatives, fillers, emulsifiers, or palm oil. Not fancy, just 100% pure chocolate.

Made to Order

We craft only after receiving your order and dispatch weekly. Our chocolate is never pre-made.

Ethical Sourcing

We work with farms that respect their people and the environment. Fair trade, always.

Vegan Friendly

Our entire range is 100% plant-based. Luxury chocolate doesn't need dairy.

Full Transparency

From farm to your hands — we share every step of the journey openly.
