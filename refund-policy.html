Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At Rare Cocoa™, we take great pride in every batch we handcraft. We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. Please read our refund and cancellation policy carefully.

1. Order Cancellations

  • Cancellations are accepted within 24 hours of placing the order, provided the order has not yet been dispatched.
  • To cancel an order, email us at rarecocoa7@gmail.com or WhatsApp us at +91 76749 31380 with your order details.
  • Once an order has been dispatched, it cannot be cancelled.

2. Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If you receive a product that is damaged, defective, or incorrect, we will resolve the issue promptly. Please:

  • Contact us within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Share a clear photo or video of the damaged or incorrect product.
  • We will offer a replacement or full refund at our discretion.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

We are unable to process refunds in the following cases:

  • Products returned without prior communication and approval.
  • Products that have been partially or fully consumed.
  • Dissatisfaction due to personal taste preferences (our chocolates use pure, natural ingredients with no artificial sweeteners or additives — flavour profiles may differ from mass-market products).
  • Claims made after 48 hours of delivery.

4. Refund Process

Approved refunds will be processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method. Depending on your bank or payment provider, it may take an additional 3–5 days to reflect in your account.

5. Custom Orders

Custom and made-to-order products (such as Make Your Own blends) are non-refundable once production has begun, except in cases of damage or error on our part.
